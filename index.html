Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

Overview

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026, organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF), brings together growers, processors, roasters, traders and buyers from across the world to celebrate Uganda's position as Africa's leading coffee exporter. Over three days at Speke Resort Munyonyo, the expo will showcase the finest Robusta and Arabica beans from the country's farming regions.

Delegates can look forward to a full programme of keynote addresses, panel discussions, cupping sessions and business-to-business meetings, alongside an exhibition floor featuring the latest in processing technology, sustainable farming and value addition. Whether you are looking to source, invest or learn, the expo is the place to build lasting partnerships in the coffee value chain.
