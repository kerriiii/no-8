Keynote Speakers

Dr. Amina Nakato

Permanent Secretary, MAAIF

Dr. Nakato leads national policy on agricultural exports and has championed coffee as a pillar of Uganda's economy. She has over twenty years of experience in agricultural development.

Mr. Joseph Okello

Chief Economist, East Africa Commodities Board

Mr. Okello analyses global commodity trends and advises governments on export strategy. His market forecasts are widely used by traders across the region.

Prof. Grace Atim

Professor of Agronomy, Makerere University

Prof. Atim researches climate-resilient coffee varieties and sustainable farming practices. She has trained thousands of smallholder farmers in improved agronomy.

Ms. Sarah Namutebi

Founder, Pearl Roasters Uganda

Ms. Namutebi built a local roastery into an award-winning specialty coffee brand. She is a leading advocate for value addition before export.

Call for Papers

We invite researchers, practitioners and industry professionals to submit abstracts on themes including sustainable production, value addition, market access, coffee technology and rural livelihoods. Abstracts should not exceed 300 words and must clearly state the objectives, methods and key findings.

Submissions must be made in English as a Word or PDF document, with the author's name, organisation and contact details on a separate cover page. Selected presenters will be given a 15-minute slot in the programme and will be notified by email.
